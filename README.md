**[Does satellite radiance measure the street lighting people actually experience?](https://vituk123.github.io/research-notes/alan-validation/)**
A measurement note testing whether VIIRS satellite night-light radiance corresponds to
residents' own reported satisfaction with street lighting, across 500+ Gauteng wards using
three rounds of the GCRO Quality of Life survey.
